# 0063 Copy rewrite: studio vocabulary, no filler (2026-11-22)

Based on the Egypt boutique-studio and gym research (see the chat summary: members/classes/class packs/freeze/intro offers, EGP and InstaPay, WhatsApp), every line of the landing page was rewritten to be concrete and short.
- Hero: "Classes, members, coaches. All in one system." Support: "Book classes, check members in, take payment and pay coaches from one system."
- Statement pills: concrete tasks only ("Take cash, card or InstaPay", "Run the coach payout", "Log every session", "Track coach attendance").
- Benefits: Check members in / Take payment any way / See who's coming / Know your numbers (one plain sentence each).
- Feature rows: bullets tightened, "clients" became "members"; coach attendance is stated as coach attendance.
- Plans: "Priced by active members and team size." Extra *members* never cost more than the next plan. Enterprise: "Custom limits, apps and integrations."
- FAQ: shorter answers, same facts and numbers; the CTA is "Talk to us" (also the hero and plan buttons); closing cards "Call us / Email us / See the plans".
- SEO description: what the product does, with the price.
Removed filler: "Take payment your way", "Stay in control", "Start small. Move up as you grow", "Everything is custom, built around you", "Let's talk", "simply", "in one place".
Not changed: the screens inside the phones still say "client" (the product's own word: Clients tab, New client), and the Privacy and Support policy pages still use "client". Prices, plan names and all rules are as before (`npm run check:content` passes).
