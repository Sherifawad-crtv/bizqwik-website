# 0035 — CTA hover: smoother rounding, white CTAs reverse

**Date:** 2026-11-11 · Direction: the hover rounding is a bit harsh, make it smoother; invert the hover colours of the white CTAs.

## Why it felt harsh
The buttons are 56px tall, so a corner radius above 28px changes nothing: the 20 to 100px move only shows its first 8px. With an ease-out curve those 8px passed in a few milliseconds.

## What changed (`components/ui/Button.tsx`)
- Hover radius target is **28px** (identical look to a full pill at this height) and the corners get their own transition, **0.5s** (`duration.enter`, `ease.standard`); transform, filter and colour keep 0.25s.
  Measured radius every 45ms after hover: 20.0, 20.2, 22.0, 24.4, 26.0, 26.8, 27.4, 27.8, 27.9, 28.0: the move is spread across the whole half second.
- The **white (secondary) CTA** reverses on hover: background `ink` (#1A1726), text `surface` (#FBFAF7), and back on leave. The violet CTA keeps its darkening hover (`primary-pressed`).
- Only CTAs (the `Button` component) are affected. Replay pill, menu button and the rest are unchanged.

## Verification (Chromium, production build)
- Computed styles: primary 20px, violet `rgb(90,65,255)`, hovered 28px, `rgb(72,50,214)`; secondary 20px, `rgb(251,250,247)` with `rgb(26,23,38)` text, hovered 28px, `rgb(26,23,38)` with `rgb(251,250,247)` text, back to the rest values after leaving. No console errors.
  `tsc`, `oxlint`, `check:motion`, `next build` pass.

## Notes
- The closing section's "See the plans" is a white CTA on a dark card; reversed it is a dark button on a slightly lighter dark card (readable text, subtle edge).
