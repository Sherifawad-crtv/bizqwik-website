## Update 2026-11-22: the colour picker motion
The brand phone now plays one motion: a colour picker (square, hue slider, HEX row, close button) rises over the app; the pointer grabs the hue handle and drags it along the slider, then the handle in the square, and the whole app takes the picked colour live, frame by frame (brand colour, the text on it, background, soft tiles, ink, the tab bar and quick actions), from the default violet to the gym's green (#718038). The picker then closes (tap on the x) and the app rests on green. Replay is back. Colours come from one hue/saturation/value (`themeFrom` in brand-app.tsx), the same function paints the still frame, so reduced motion shows the finished green home. The old splash, brand form and booking/plan/top-up sheets are still in the markup, unused.
