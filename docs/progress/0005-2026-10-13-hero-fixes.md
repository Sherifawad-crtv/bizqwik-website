# 0005 — Hero decluttered, true-size screen, navbar centred and post-hero

**Date:** 2026-10-13 · Feedback on 0003/0004.

## What changed
1. **Hero noise removed.** Only title, subtitle and the two CTAs remain; eyebrow, fine
   print and five-app proof strip deleted (components and `content/hero.ts` fields).
2. **Navbar: the pill is the fixed centre.** Logo and CTA are absolutely positioned at
   the edges and travel to sit 12px beside the stationary pill. The pill's centre is the
   viewport centre in both states (it used to drift because the bar's width animated).
3. **Navbar change starts after the hero.** `nav/motion.ts` reads the hero's pin end via
   `HERO_PIN_ID`; the hero refreshes first (`refreshPriority: 10`). Without a hero it
   starts at the top as before.
4. **Phone screen at true iPhone Pro Max size.** Rebuilt `FounderHome.tsx` in a 440 x 957
   pt design space (the frame's screen aspect) with the product's real layout numbers
   (safe areas, 16px padding, 64px avatar, KPI grid, chart port, 64px nav pill + 52px
   tabs, 64px FAB). Pieces are flow children; docking positions are measured, not
   hand-placed. `PIECES` table became `SCATTER` (scatter only). FAB is now a fixed 64px
   circle (it was being squeezed into a pill by flex shrink) and a separate scatter piece.
   Active nav tab uses the product's filled home glyph.

## Verification (Chromium, production build)
- Navbar, on a temporary test page with content after the hero (deleted): pill centre 720
  at scroll 0, mid-hero, hero end and fully scrolled; logo/CTA unchanged until the hero's
  pin end (1710px at 900px viewport), then travel in and land exactly 12px from the pill;
  returns to the wide state on scroll up.
- Hero at 1440x900, 1920x1080, 390x844: empty phone with scattered pieces at the start,
  true-size founder home at the end; no overflow; no console errors.
- `tsc`, `oxlint`, `next build` pass.
