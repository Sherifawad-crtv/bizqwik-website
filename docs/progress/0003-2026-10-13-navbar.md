# 0003 — Navbar: wide ⇄ narrow, mobile pill + sheet

**Date:** 2026-10-13 · **Stage:** first real component. Reference: three Nefin
screenshots (desktop wide, desktop narrow, mobile), restyled in Bizqwik tokens.

## What changed
- `src/components/nav/Navbar.tsx` (mounted in the root layout) and
  `src/components/nav/motion.ts` (GSAP). Behaviour and measurements are in
  DESIGN-SYSTEM.md, "Navbar".
- Desktop: wide to narrow cluster scrubbed over the first 160px of scroll;
  wordmark crossfades to the emblem tile; sliding active-link indicator.
- Mobile: glass pill + rounded-square menu button (CSS two-line icon morphing to X);
  glass sheet with links, Talk to us, phone, email.
- `src/components/ui/Button.tsx` (primary/secondary link buttons).
- `src/lib/useActiveSection.ts`; `src/motion/lenis.ts` (shared Lenis handle so
  the menu can lock scroll).
- Skip link, `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`, `inert` while the sheet is closed,
  `aria-current` on the active link. `/foundation` got `id="main"` and `id="top"`.

## Why
The navbar is the first thing that sets the motion language, so it carries the
scroll-scrubbed GSAP + Lenis feel from the reference. Content (labels, CTA,
contact) comes from `src/content`.

## Verification (Chromium, production build)
- Desktop geometry: wide logo x=80 / CTA right edge 1360 (container edges);
  narrow cluster 420 to 1021, centred at 720, 12px gaps; returns exactly on scroll up.
- Reload while scrolled mid-page lands in the narrow state.
- Active indicator tracked The apps, Plans, Add-ons, FAQ on a temporary test route
  with real sections (route deleted); hidden at the top.
- Anchor click scrolled to section minus 96px. This caught a bug: the offset was
  applied twice (CSS scroll-padding + Lenis), landing 192px short; Lenis offset removed.
- Reduced motion: instant switch at 80px; Lenis off.
- Mobile menu opens/closes, Escape closes, `aria-expanded` follows.
- 360, 430, 768, 1023, 1024, 1280, 1920: no horizontal overflow; swap at 1024.
- `tsc`, `oxlint`, `next build`, `check:content` pass; no console errors.
